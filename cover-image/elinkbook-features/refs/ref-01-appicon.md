# Reference 01: elinkBook App Icon v2 (`appicon_v2.jpg`)

- **File**: `refs/ref-01-appicon.jpg`
- **Core Visual Metaphor**:
  - An open classical hardcover book with emerald/jade green cloth cover, gold-trimmed corners, and golden binder rings.
  - A magical miniature world emerging directly from the open book pages:
    - **Glowing Golden Pagoda / Pavilion (金殿閣)**: A multi-tiered traditional Chinese pagoda pavilion with flying eaves, glowing intensely with warm golden lantern light from inside.
    - **Golden Winding River of Light (金色流光溪水)**: A radiant river of golden mist and light flowing out from the base of the glowing pagoda across the pages.
    - **Left Page**: Ink wash bamboo grove (翠竹), swirling auspicious misty clouds (祥雲), and vertical Traditional Chinese poetry text.
    - **Right Page**: Serene landscape with a bonsai-like pine tree (青松), green hillocks, blooming white flowers, and traditional ornamental book borders.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Soft Jade / Celadon Green (翡翠綠、竹青、粉青)
  - Radiant Golden Amber (溫暖金光、琉璃金、琥珀光)
  - Pure Ivory / Xuan Rice Paper Cream (宣紙米白)
  - Deep Pine Green and Gold Foil Leaf (墨綠與金箔邊角)
- **Art Style & Technique**:
  - High-end fairytale watercolor and delicate fine ink linework (水彩國風工筆插畫).
  - Poetic, magical, serene: "書中自有黃金屋，書中自有山水天地".
  - Soft textured watercolor paper background.
- **Usage**: `direct` & `style` — The new cover MUST directly inherit this exact concept, aesthetic, color palette, and visual elements.
